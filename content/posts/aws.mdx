I've been deep in AWS Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) prep for a while now working through a structured study roadmap, practice exams, spaced repetition, the usual grind of turning acronyms into actual understanding. So when the chance came up to visit the AWS Skill Centre in Cape Town, it felt like a good way to step away from the screen and see some of this in a more physical, hands-on setting.

## Why go in person

Studying for a cloud certification is, ironically, a very screen-bound activity. Diagrams, practice questions, documentation — all abstract until you're standing somewhere built specifically to make those concepts tangible. A skill centre visit is a chance to ask questions out loud, see how concepts like regions, availability zones, and shared responsibility get explained to a room instead of a slide, and get a sense of the broader AWS ecosystem beyond exam objectives.

## What it means for the certification push

None of this replaces the actual studying — the practice exams and active recall are still doing the heavy lifting toward CLF-C02. But a visit like this is a good checkpoint: a reminder of why the material matters beyond passing an exam, and useful context for topics that can feel dry on paper (billing and pricing, service categories, the sheer breadth of what "cloud" actually covers).
